The Case Against AI Optimism
By Sarah Chen  ·  The Atlantic  ·  April 2025
For years, technologists have promised that artificial intelligence would solve humanity's most pressing problems — from climate change to cancer to poverty.
By convincing policymakers that AI is on the cusp of transformative breakthroughs, technologists are displacing urgency from interventions that we know work.
Consider the healthcare domain. AI diagnostic tools have been celebrated as the future of medicine. Yet a 2024 meta-analysis found that accuracy degrades significantly in real clinical environments.
